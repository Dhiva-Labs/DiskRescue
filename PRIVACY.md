# Privacy

DiskRescue works entirely on your computer.

- It never connects to the internet. There are no accounts, no telemetry, no
  crash reports and no update checks (the Microsoft Store updates its edition).
- It reads only the drives, image files and media files you choose, and
  writes only to the folders you choose: recovered files, repaired copies,
  drive images, their progress maps and a report.
- Nothing it reads or recovers is sent anywhere or shared with anyone,
  including Dhiva-Labs.
- The desktop app remembers, on your computer only, its window size and the
  last few results folders you opened, so it can offer them again.
